# Code of conduct

Yowl is a small project. Keep it a place where anyone can report a bug or send
a fix without being made to regret it.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith, especially toward people new to the project
  or to open source.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and say why when you decline
  one yourself.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or
  background.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm
  themselves.
- Publishing someone's private information, including photos taken by Yowl,
  without their consent.
- Repeatedly pursuing someone who has asked you to stop.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to jernejkocica@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer
and kept confidential. Depending on what happened, the response may be a
warning, removal of comments, or a ban from the project's spaces.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent
the project. It is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
