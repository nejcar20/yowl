Luka and I do our best work in bars.

Not the drinking part. The part where you take a laptop somewhere with noise and
other people in it, and the work actually gets done. We have been doing it for
years.

The trouble with working in a bar is that eventually you need the bathroom.

And then you run the calculation every person with a laptop has run at some
point: is it worth it? Do I pack the whole thing up for four minutes? Do I ask
the stranger at the next table to "keep an eye on it" — a stranger who, if
someone actually picked it up and walked out, would almost certainly do nothing?

We talked about that problem for a long time before I finally built it.

Yowl is a menu-bar app for Mac. You arm it when you get up from the table. If
someone unplugs the charger, closes the lid, or lifts the machine, it screams at
full volume — over mute, over connected headphones — locks the screen, and
photographs whoever is sitting in front of it. The photo arrives on your phone.

It will not get your laptop back. Find My does that. What it does is make taking
your laptop loud, public and photographed in a room full of people, which is a
different job — and in a bar, probably the more useful one.

The hard part was not the one I expected. Making a Mac scream is easy. Making it
keep screaming after someone closes the lid is not: macOS suspends almost
everything within a second or two of the lid shutting, and on battery it means
it. That single problem took longer than every other feature put together, and I
was told more than once that it simply could not be done on macOS.

It is free, MIT licensed, and the whole source is public. The app asks for your
camera. You should not take my word for what it does with it — and now you do
not have to.

dontstealmylaptop.com

If you work out of bars and cafés: I would like to know whether it holds up at
your table.
