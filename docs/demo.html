What the app is doing
  1. Nothing yet — press Arm.
This is a walkthrough, not the app. The screenshots are real, and so are the numbers in the log — the volume really is re-forced every second, the photographs really do start ten seconds before the trigger. But a web page cannot take your camera or make your Mac scream, so the sounds and the lock are described rather than performed.

Everything you can change

  • Every trigger is a checkbox. The charger is on by default. The camera and the lid sensor are opt-in, and the lid one only appears on Macs that have the hinge sensor.
  • Every response too. Siren, screen lock, photographs, phone alert — each can be switched off, and the app refuses to arm if you switch off everything that could catch a theft.
  • Test sensitivity shows the live motion score at your own desk, so you can see whether your background defeats the detection before you trust it.
  • Grace period defaults to none — the siren fires the instant the charger is pulled. Give yourself seconds if you would rather.
  • Phone alerts pair by scanning a code, with a separate path for iPhone and Android because only Android can open the ntfy app from a scan.
  • No passcode required. Unlocking your Mac is the disarm. Set one only if you turn the screen lock off.
