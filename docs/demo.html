Your laptop, unattended

Yowl sits in the menu bar with no window and no Dock icon. Nothing is watching until you press Arm — the camera is not running and nothing is being recorded.

What you can change

TriggersThe charger is on by default. The camera and the lid sensor are opt-in, and the lid one only appears on Macs that have the hinge sensor.
ResponsesSiren, screen lock, photographs, phone alert — each switches off independently, and arming is refused if you switch off everything that could catch a theft.
SensitivityA live motion score at your own desk, so you can see whether your background defeats the detection before you trust it.
Grace periodNone by default — the siren fires the instant the charger is pulled. Give yourself seconds if you would rather.
Phone alertsPaired by scanning a code, with separate paths for iPhone and Android, because only Android can open the ntfy app from a scan.
PasscodeNot required. Unlocking your Mac is the disarm. Set one only if you switch the screen lock off.

This page is a walkthrough, not the app. The screenshots are the shipping interface and the timings are the real ones — the volume really is taken back every second, the photographs really do reach ten seconds back. But a web page cannot hold your camera or make your Mac scream, so the sound and the lock are described rather than performed.
