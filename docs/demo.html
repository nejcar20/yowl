What you can change

Triggers
Charger on by default. Camera and lid sensor are opt-in; the lid one only appears on Macs that have the hinge sensor.
Responses
Siren, screen lock, photographs, phone alert — each switches off on its own, and arming is refused if nothing is left that could catch a theft.
Sensitivity
A live motion score at your own desk, so you can see whether your background defeats the detection before trusting it.
Grace period
None by default. The siren fires the instant the charger is pulled.
Phone alerts
Paired by scanning a code, with different paths for iPhone and Android — only Android can open the ntfy app from a scan.
Passcode
Not required. Unlocking your Mac is the disarm.

This page is a walkthrough, not the app. The photographs are the shipping interface and the timings are the real ones — the volume really is taken back every second, the photographs really do reach ten seconds back. A web page cannot hold your camera or make your Mac scream, so the sound and the lock are written down rather than performed.
